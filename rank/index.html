Claude Connector Rank

See where any connector ranks in Claude's connector directory, how it moved, and who leads each category. Ask Claude in plain language.

server url

https://mcp.toolmonsters.com/mcp

Streamable HTTP. No sign-in, no API key. Read-only.

Tools

ToolWhat it returns
get_connector_rankA connector's popularity rank and trending rank in Claude's directory, and how its rank changed since tracking began.
compare_connectorsUp to 10 connectors side by side, best rank first.
top_connectorsThe most popular connectors overall or in one category (sales-and-marketing, data-analytics, developer-tools, productivity, financial-services...).

Try it

  • "Where does Notion rank in Claude's connector directory?"
  • "Compare Apollo, ZoomInfo and Lusha in Claude's directory."
  • "Who are the top 10 data analytics connectors in Claude right now?"

Data

Ranks come from the public listing at claude.com/marketplace, refreshed every 3 hours, with daily history since October 22, 2026. Popularity rank orders connectors by Claude's published popularity score; trending rank orders them by its trending score. Tool Monsters is not affiliated with Anthropic.
