05how claude picks a connector

Three steps. The listing decides all three.

step 1Claude searches

It turns the request into a few keywords and searches the connector directory.

step 2The directory answers

Up to 10 connectors whose name, one-liner, tools or description match. Popularity breaks ties.

step 3Claude chooses

Reading only the name, the one-liner and the first 8 tool names.
